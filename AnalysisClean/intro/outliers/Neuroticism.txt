Method: Tukey's fences

How to calculate Q1 and Q3? go to Q1 and Q3 step by step calculation.
Q1 = 0.5.
Q3 = 0.625.
IRQ = Q3 - Q1 = 0.625 - 0.5 = 0.125.
Lower = Q1 - k * IRQ = 0.5 - 1.5*0.125 = 0.3125.
Upper = Q3 + k * IRQ = 0.625 + 1.5*0.125 = 0.8125.
